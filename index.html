✨ About The Sweet Lab

The Sweet Lab was created as a student business concept focused on fun, affordable sweet treats. We wanted to combine two things people love — refreshing smoothies and chocolate-covered strawberries — while giving customers different flavors and combo options. Our goal is simple: make every order feel like a little treat worth enjoying.
